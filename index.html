UPPER EAST • CULTURE & ADVENTURE

Tongo Hills & Tengzug

A distinctive cultural landscape of rock formations, caves and sacred Talensi shrines. The Ghana Museums and Monuments Board identifies Tengzuk as a cultural landscape about 15 km southeast of Bolgatanga.

UPPER EAST • WILDLIFE & HERITAGE

Paga

Paga combines the sacred crocodile pond with heritage experiences including the Pikworo Slave Camp and guided village tours. Visit Ghana notes that early morning and the dry season can be good times for crocodile viewing.

Look forCrocodiles • Heritage
ExperienceVillage & cultural tour

SAVANNAH • WILDLIFE & NATURE

Mole National Park

Ghana's largest wildlife refuge features Guinea savanna, elephants and more than 300 recorded bird species. Nearby Mognori and Larabanga add community and heritage experiences.
